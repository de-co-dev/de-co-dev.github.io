Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Who we are

Pill Reminder (“the app”) is operated by Dennys Garcia Collins (“we”). For any question about your data, write to support@pillreminder.app.

2. What data we collect

  • Account: name, email and password (the password is stored encrypted by our authentication provider; we never see it).
  • Profile: date of birth and, if you add them, weight and height.
  • Health: the medications, doses, schedules and dose history you enter.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos or usage data for advertising.

3. Health data

Information about your medications is sensitive data. You provide it voluntarily and we only use it to make the app work. By entering it, you give us your explicit consent to process it for that sole purpose.

4. How we use your data

To schedule your reminders and alarms, show your history and maintain your account. We don’t sell your data, use it for advertising or build commercial profiles.

5. Who we share it with

Only with providers needed to run the app: our server and authentication provider (Supabase) and, if you sign in with them, Apple or Google, which only send us your name and email. We may also disclose it if required by law.

6. How we protect it

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored on your iPhone and on secured servers. No system is 100% secure; if an incident affects your data, we’ll notify you as required by law.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your data from our servers; technical backups are erased within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can view and correct your data in the app, delete your account in Settings → Privacy and data, withdraw your consent, or ask us about your data at support@pillreminder.app.

9. Minimum age

The app is only for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from minors; if we find an account belonging to a minor, we’ll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is important, we’ll let you know in the app before it takes effect.

Questions? Write to support@pillreminder.app

NOTE: this privacy policy is a first draft version and is not applicable or legally binding in this version 0.0.1. For reference and testing only.
